# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Property owners, building admins, accountants, and trusted operators who manage rental buildings, units, tenants, payments, expenses, ownership shares, and reports. They use the app during operational workflows where accuracy, clarity, and fast lookup matter more than decorative presentation.

## Product Purpose

Rentals V2 is a bilingual English/Arabic rental property management system. It exists to centralize building and property records, tenant data, monthly payments, receipts, expenses, ownership distribution, and yearly financial reports. Success means operators can confidently enter, review, and report financial and property data with minimal ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, reliable, precise. The interface should feel like an operational tool: calm, structured, legible, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy SaaS dashboards, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, oversized marketing-style typography, ornamental motion, and low-density layouts that slow repeated admin work.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize financial accuracy and scannability over visual novelty.
- Keep bilingual and RTL/LTR behavior first-class on every screen.
- Make actions explicit, reversible where possible, and consistent across modules.
- Use restrained color for hierarchy, state, and selection rather than decoration.
- Favor dense but readable operational layouts for repeated daily use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default to WCAG AA expectations. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, adequate touch targets, reduced-motion support, and robust Arabic/English localization.
